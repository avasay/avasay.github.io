About

Alejandrio Vasay

Software Engineer & Blogger

Welcome to Coder Schmoder — a tech blog where I share insights, tutorials, and opinions from my 25+ years of software development experience, primarily in the .NET ecosystem.

I started this blog to cut through the noise and deliver practical, experience-driven content. No fluff, no clickbait — just real articles about real problems that developers face every day.

What I Write About

  • .NET & Blazor — Tutorials, tips, and deep dives into Microsoft's web framework
  • C++ — From language history to modern best practices
  • AI & Automation — How artificial intelligence is reshaping software development and the job market
  • Developer Tools — Practical guides to getting more done with less friction

My Background

I studied under Bjarne Stroustrup at Texas A&M University and have spent over two decades building software with .NET, C++, and everything in between. I've seen tooling shifts from IntelliSense to Roslyn analyzers to AI-assisted coding — and I write about all of it.
