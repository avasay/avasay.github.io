Conclusion

Blazor already gives you a clean and efficient way to handle component communication without adding unnecessary complexity. By sticking to [Parameter] for passing data down and EventCallback for sending data up, you keep your components loosely coupled, easier to maintain, and more reusable.

Resist the urge to introduce shared state or services too early—those are best reserved for more complex scenarios like app-wide state management. For most cases, this simple pattern is all you need.
